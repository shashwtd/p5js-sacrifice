# Echo Orb

this is a game where you are an orb of light.. you trace your path, wherever you go and theres always some evil shapes in your way. You cant ignore them, they get bigger and stronger. To battle them, you must turn your pathway trace into a shield that explodes all enemeies..
... HOWEVER THIS COMES AT A COST OF YOU BEING VULNERABLE TO ATTACKS..

be careful, enjoy the game. whoever lasts longest shall win!
